## Key Themes Across All Content

### 1. On-Device AI is the Future
Model sizes have collapsed (175B → 1B), quantization is solved, Apple Silicon is exceptional. 2026 is the inflection point.

### 2. Engineering Discipline Matters
On-device ML has unique constraints: no cloud safety net, system-level fragility, silent failures. Careful engineering required.

### 3. User Control > Vendor Lock-In
We support 4 STT + 8 LLM models because users should choose their speed/quality tradeoff, not vendors.

### 4. Testing & Documentation Multiply Effectiveness
20 distinct lessons prove this. Test discipline prevents regressions, clear docs enable onboarding.
